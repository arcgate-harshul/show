About Jetha Kitchen Equipments & Designings

Our History

Founded and managed by Mr. Mahesh Jetha, Jetha Kitchen Equipments & Designings is a premier manufacturer, supplier, and trader of robust commercial kitchen equipment based in Udaipur, Rajasthan. We specialize in providing comprehensive kitchen solutions that bring efficiency to commercial culinary environments.

Infrastructure

We operate out of a highly equipped manufacturing facility located in the Gudli Industrial Area, Udaipur. Our corporate operations and direct client interactions are handled at our main office located Opposite Dalal Petrol Pump, B N College Road, Sevashram Road, Udaipur (313001).

Our Mission

To deliver stainless steel excellence. Be it commercial setups, customized modular kitchens, catering equipment, deep fryers, or water coolers, our mission is to deliver durable, hygienic, and expertly designed products that help businesses succeed.
